Privacy Policy – ScaleGoal Tracker

ScaleGoal Tracker does not collect, store, or share personal data with external servers.

All data entered in the application, including weight records and settings, is stored only on the user's device. No data is transmitted, uploaded, or shared outside the device.

The application does not require user accounts and does not use external databases.

ScaleGoal Tracker does not use analytics services, advertising services, or third-party tracking systems.

Notifications are used only to remind the user to record weight, according to the settings chosen by the user. These notifications work locally on the device.

Data usage

All information remains private and is accessible only to the user. The developer does not have access to any personal data.

Third-party services

The application only uses standard Android system services required for normal functionality. No personal data is collected or transmitted by the developer.

Children’s privacy

This application is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application functionality changes in the future.
